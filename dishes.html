Famous Dishes

Some of the foods that Bangladesh is best known for.

Kacchi Biryani

Kacchi biryani is a famous dish from Old Dhaka. "Kacchi" means raw: the raw meat is marinated in yogurt and spices, then layered with rice and cooked slowly together in a sealed pot. Dhaka-style kacchi usually has potatoes in it. It is a must at weddings and big parties, and it is often served with borhani, a spicy yogurt drink.

Photo: Solomon The Magnifico, CC BY-SA 4.0

Fuchka

Fuchka is one of the most popular street foods in Bangladesh. It is a small, hollow, crispy shell filled with spicy mashed chickpeas and potatoes, then dipped in sour tamarind water and eaten in one bite. In other countries it is called pani puri or golgappa.

Photo: Zenith zian, CC0 (public domain)

More Dishes to Know

Ilish Bhaja

Fried hilsa fish. Hilsa (ilish) is the national fish of Bangladesh and is often fried in mustard oil and eaten with plain rice. You can see it on the home page photo.

Bhorta

Bhorta means "mashed." Potatoes, eggplant, lentils or dried fish are mashed with mustard oil, onion and green chili. It is simple home food eaten with rice.

Dal

A thin lentil soup, usually made with red lentils (masoor dal). Almost every Bangladeshi meal has dal on the side.

Mishti Doi

Sweet yogurt made with milk and sugar or date molasses, set in clay pots. Bogura is a town famous for it.
