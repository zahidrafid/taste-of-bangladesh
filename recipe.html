Easy Recipe: Khichuri

A warm rice and lentil dish that is easy enough for a beginner.

What Is Khichuri?

Khichuri is rice and lentils cooked together with spices in one pot. In Bangladesh it is a comfort food, and many families make it on rainy days. It is often eaten with fried egg, fried eggplant, or beef or chicken curry.

This version is bhuna khichuri, a thicker style where the rice and lentils are fried in spices before the water goes in.

  • Serves: 4
  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Cook time: 30 minutes
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Tip: check off each item as you get it ready.

Steps

  1. Put the moong dal in a dry pan on medium heat. Stir it for 3 to 4 minutes until it smells nutty and turns light golden. This gives the khichuri more flavor.
  2. Wash the rice and the roasted dal together 2 or 3 times, until the water is mostly clear. Drain well.
  3. Heat the oil in a heavy pot. Add the bay leaves and the sliced onion. Fry for about 5 minutes until the onion is soft and light brown.
  4. Add the ginger paste, garlic paste, turmeric, cumin and chili powder with a splash of water. Stir for 1 to 2 minutes so the spices cook and do not burn.
  5. Add the drained rice and dal. Stir gently for 2 to 3 minutes so every grain is coated with the spices.
  6. Pour in the hot water. Add the salt and the green chilies. Bring it to a boil.
  7. Turn the heat to low, cover the pot, and cook for about 20 minutes. Stir once or twice so it does not stick. Add a little more hot water if it gets too dry.
  8. When the rice and dal are soft, turn off the heat. Stir in the ghee if you are using it. Let it rest for 5 minutes, then serve hot with a fried egg or fried eggplant.
