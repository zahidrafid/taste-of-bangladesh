Why Bangladeshi Food?

Bangladesh is a country in South Asia with many rivers. Because of all that water, rice and fish are the main foods in most homes. There is even an old Bengali saying, Machhe-bhate Bangali, which means "fish and rice make a Bengali."

Bangladeshi cooking uses mustard oil, fresh green chilies and a special five-seed spice mix called panch phoron. Meals are usually eaten with rice in the middle of the plate and small portions of curry, lentils (dal) and mashed vegetables (bhorta) around it.
